## 1. Mandatory Attendance Threshold

1. **Minimum Attendance Requirement:**
   - Every enrolled student must maintain a minimum aggregate attendance of **75%** in each registered theory and practical course per semester.
   - Attendance is computed from the first instructional day of the semester to the last working day.

2. **Impact on Placement and Examination Eligibility:**
   - Attendance below the 75% threshold directly affects student eligibility for end-semester examinations and campus placements.
   - Students falling below 75% without authorized condonation are designated as "Debarred due to Lack of Attendance" (ND - No Detention Condoned).
   - Mandatory attendance verification is conducted by the Academic Affairs committee prior to campus placement participation and hall ticket issuance.

3. **Medical and Sports Condonation:**
   - Condonation for attendance between 65% and 74% may be granted solely on valid medical grounds or official representation of the institution in state/national academic or athletic competitions.
   - Medical certificates must be submitted within 3 working days of returning to campus.
   - Attendance below 65% cannot be condoned under any circumstances, requiring the student to repeat the course.

---

## 2. Monitoring and Grievance Mechanism

- Biometric and digital portal attendance records are updated daily.
- Warning notices are issued at 80% and 75% markers to students and faculty mentors.
