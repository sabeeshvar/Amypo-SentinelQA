---

## 1. Internal Assessment Framework

1. **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA):**
   - Each course evaluation consists of 40% Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) weightage and 60% End-Semester Examination (ESE) weightage.
   - CIA marks are composed of two mid-term written examinations (20 marks), weekly assignments and quizzes (10 marks), and lab/mini-project presentations (10 marks).
   - A student must secure a minimum of 50% in internal assessments to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.

---

## 2. Semester Examination Rules

1. **Passing Criteria:**
   - To successfully pass a course, a candidate must obtain at least 45% in the End-Semester Examination and an aggregate (CIA + ESE) of 50% (Grade Point 5.0 / Letter Grade E).
   - Revaluation and answer script review requests must be filed within 10 calendar days of grade publication.

2. **Supplementary Examinations:**
   - Arrear examinations for odd-semester courses are conducted during the regular odd-semester schedule; even-semester arrears are held during even semesters.
   - Fast-track summer supplementary exams are offered exclusively to graduating final-year students.

---

## 3. Academic Progression & Credit Requirements

1. **Progression Criteria:**
   - Progression to the 3rd year (5th semester) requires earning at least 70% of total credits prescribed for the 1st year.
   - Progression to the 4th year (7th semester) requires clearing all credits and active arrears from the 1st year and earning at least 75% of credits prescribed up to the 3rd year.

2. **Grading and Honors Classification:**
   - First Class with Distinction: CGPA &ge; 8.5 with no standing backlogs in any semester.
   - First Class: CGPA &ge; 6.5.
   - Second Class: CGPA between 5.0 and 6.49.
