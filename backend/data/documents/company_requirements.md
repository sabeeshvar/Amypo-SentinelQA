## 1. Company Eligibility Matrix

The following sample company recruitment eligibility standards apply for campus drives:

### Company A (Tier-1 Core Software & Technology)

- **Minimum CGPA:** **7.5** (No rounding permitted)
- **Active Arrears:** **No active arrears** (0 backlogs at time of drive)
- **Minimum Attendance:** **75%** mandatory institutional attendance
- **Eligible Disciplines:** Computer Science & Engineering (CSE), Information Technology (IT), Electronics & Communication (ECE), and Electrical & Electronics (EEE).
- **Hiring Roles:** Software Engineer, Systems Developer, Cloud Infrastructure Specialist.
- **Selection Rounds:** Online Coding Assessment (LeetCode Medium equivalent), Technical Architecture Interview, Behavioral Fitment Round.

---

### Company B (Global Consulting & IT Solutions)

- **Minimum CGPA:** **7.0**
- **Maximum Allowed Active Arrears:** **0** (All previous backlogs must be fully cleared)
- **Minimum Attendance:** **70%**
- **Eligible Disciplines:** All Engineering and Technology disciplines (Open to all streams).
- **Hiring Roles:** Associate Consultant, Software Analyst, Data Quality Specialist.
- **Selection Rounds:** Quantitative & Logical Aptitude Test, Group Discussion / Case Study, Managerial Interview.

---

### Company C (Hardware & Embedded Systems)

- **Minimum CGPA:** **8.0**
- **Active Arrears:** **0**
- **Minimum Attendance:** **80%**
- **Eligible Disciplines:** ECE, EEE, Robotics & Automation.
- **Hiring Roles:** Embedded Firmware Engineer, VLSI Design Trainee.
