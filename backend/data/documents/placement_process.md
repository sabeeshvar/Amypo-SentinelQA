## 1. Overview of Placement Lifecycle

The campus recruitment cycle is organized into seven sequential stages:

```text
[1. Placement Registration] ──> [2. Resume Submission] ──> [3. Eligibility Verification]
        │
        ▼
[4. Pre-Placement Training] ──> [5. Aptitude / Technical Assessment]
        │
        ▼
[6. Technical & HR Interviews] ──> [7. Final Selection & Offer Letter]
```

---

## 2. Detailed Stages of the Placement Process

### Stage 1: Placement Registration

- Eligible students must complete mandatory online registration via the Institutional Placement Portal at the start of the final academic year.
- Late submissions or unregistered candidates will not be permitted to attend recruitment drives.

### Stage 2: Resume Submission

- Registered students must upload standardized institutional-format resumes along with validated academic transcripts.
- Digital verification of academic marks, project repositories, and certified skill badges is required.

### Stage 3: Eligibility Verification

- The Placement Cell automatically audits the student records against company requirements (CGPA &ge; 7.5, Attendance &ge; 75%, 0 active arrears).
- Verified candidate shortlists are published prior to each company visit.

### Stage 4: Pre-Placement Training

- Mandatory pre-placement workshops covering quantitative aptitude, data structures & algorithms, problem-solving, soft skills, and mock technical interviews.
- Minimum 80% attendance in training sessions is required to maintain placement portal access.

### Stage 5: Aptitude and Technical Assessment

- Online or proctored on-campus screening rounds including quantitative reasoning, logical ability, domain coding challenges, and verbal comprehension.
- Cutoffs are determined by individual participating companies.

### Stage 6: Interview Process

- Shortlisted candidates undergo one or more rounds of technical evaluations, system design or domain problem-solving, and managerial/HR behavioral interviews.

### Stage 7: Final Selection & Offer Acceptance

- Companies declare results and issue Letters of Intent (LOI) / Offer Letters through the Placement Office.
- Candidates must accept or decline within 48 hours in compliance with the institutional Dream / Super-Dream Offer policy.
