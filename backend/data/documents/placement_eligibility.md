## 1. General Eligibility Criteria

All undergraduate and postgraduate students seeking campus placement opportunities must satisfy the following core baseline criteria:

1. **Minimum CGPA Requirement:**
   - A minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of **7.5** on a 10.0 scale across all completed semesters with no downward rounding.

2. **Minimum Attendance Requirement:**
   - A minimum aggregate attendance of **75%** across all registered academic subjects and mandatory laboratory sessions up to the qualifying semester.

3. **Active Arrears & Standing Backlogs:**
   - Students with **active arrears are strictly not eligible** to register for or participate in any campus recruitment drives.
   - All previous course backlogs must be cleared prior to the commencement of placement registration.
   - Maximum standing arrears permitted: **0**.

4. **Company-Specific Eligibility Criteria:**
   - In addition to the baseline institutional criteria, students must satisfy company-specific eligibility requirements including higher CGPA cutoffs, 10th/12th academic percentage thresholds, or discipline restrictions specified by individual visiting recruiters.

---

## 2. Disqualification & Debarment Policy

Students will be disqualified from placement services under the following circumstances:

- Providing false academic or personal credentials during registration.
- Active disciplinary probation or violations of the institutional student code of conduct.
- Failure to maintain the 75% attendance threshold during the final graduating year.
