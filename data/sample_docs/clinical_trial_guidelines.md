# Clinical Trial Protocol & Drug Safety Verification Guidelines (CTP-2026-V3)

## 1. Study Cohort & Dosage Schedule
1.1. **Dosage Regimen**: Eligible adult participants in Phase 3 Trial (Cohort A) will receive an oral dose of 50mg Compound-Q2 daily for 12 consecutive weeks. Cohort B will receive an identical matched placebo.
1.2. **Exclusion Criteria**: Patients with a history of acute liver impairment (ALT/AST > 3x upper limit of normal) or renal failure (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m2) are strictly excluded from participation.
1.3. **Pregnancy & Contraception**: All participants of childbearing potential must maintain dual-method contraception throughout the 12-week dosing period plus a 30-day post-trial monitoring phase.

## 2. Adverse Event (AE) Reporting Protocols
2.1. **Serious Adverse Events (SAE)**: Any SAE (including hospitalization, life-threatening symptoms, or significant disability) must be reported to the Principal Investigator and Institutional Review Board (IRB) within 24 hours of occurrence.
2.2. **Non-Serious Adverse Events**: Non-serious events must be logged in the electronic data capture (EDC) system within 5 business days.
2.3. **Trial Halting Criteria**: If more than 3 participants in Cohort A experience Grade 4 hepatotoxicity, dosing is immediately suspended pending Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) review.
